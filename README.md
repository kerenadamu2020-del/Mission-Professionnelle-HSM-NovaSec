# Mission Professionnelle — Projet Paperless RH
### HSM-Bioclinique × NovaSec Consulting (School Project)

## Project Overview
A school-based professional mission simulating a cybersecurity and digital transformation consulting engagement for a fictional hospital — **Hôpital Sainte-Marie Laboratoire Bioclinique (HSM-Bioclinique)**.

The project covers the full scoping and risk analysis of a **Paperless HR project**, aiming to digitise and secure employee payslip management.


## My Role
**AMOA Consultant — Business Processes & Organisation**
- Business process mapping (HR & Paperless workflows)
- Stakeholder engagement and requirements gathering
- Co-authoring the improvement roadmap
- Digital transformation optimisation


## Methodology & Frameworks
- **EBIOS Risk Manager** (ANSSI 2018) — 5 workshops
- **ISO 27001** — Security controls & governance
- **RGPD / GDPR** — Data compliance
- **eIDAS** — Electronic signature & digital vault
- **NIS2 / DORA** — Cyber resilience reference
