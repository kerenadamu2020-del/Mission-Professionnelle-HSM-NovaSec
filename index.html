About the Project

Context

A professional mission simulating a cybersecurity and digital transformation consulting engagement for a fictional hospital — Hôpital Sainte-Marie Laboratoire Bioclinique (HSM-Bioclinique). The project covers the full scoping and risk analysis of a Paperless HR initiative, aiming to digitise and secure employee payslip management.

EBIOS RM ISO 27001 RGPD eIDAS NIS2 Healthcare AMOA

My Role

AMOA Consultant — Business Processes & Organisation. Responsible for process mapping, stakeholder engagement, and co-authoring the improvement roadmap.

Key Risk Finding

Two critical risks identified (score 20/25 and 16/25): absence of MFA and weak access management — both addressed in the remediation plan.
